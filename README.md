## What it is

Cresca is a native iOS gym tracker that turns your workout history into coaching. You log sets, reps, effort, and rest. Cresca computes estimated 1RM, load progression, plateau detection, workload ratios, and per-muscle recovery, then explains them in plain language.

The design principle behind every feature: **deterministic code computes the numbers, a small on-device language model only rephrases them.** No number you see is invented by an AI. Your training log never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

## Features

### Logging
- Quick-start blank sessions, unlimited personal routines, and ready-made templates (for example StrongLifts 5×5).
- Active Workout view with live total volume, elapsed time, set progression, and a global progress bar.
- Set types (warm-up, normal, drop, failure), RPE capture after each working set, and last-session placeholders.
- Rest timer with auto-tuning from RPE, surfaced as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
- Auto-generated warm-up ramps from your target working weight.
- Supersets, mid-session routine edits with a template-update prompt on completion.

### Coaching, computed locally
- Estimated 1RM (Brzycki and Epley), rounded to loadable weights.
- Progression engine: when target reps clear at low effort across recent sessions, Cresca suggests a micro-load or an extra rep.
- Plateau detection with one-tap variation swaps or deload suggestions.
- Acute-to-chronic workload ratio with honest confidence labelling.
- Strain and readiness scoring from logged work plus Apple Health signals (heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep).
- Per-muscle fatigue and recovery heatmap on an anatomical front and back diagram.
- Session recaps and a daily Coach's Pick, generated by an on-device model and persisted into History.

### AI coach, two engines
- Apple Foundation Models on supported devices, plus a Gemma model running through MLX on the GPU for devices and questions that need more.
- Ask the coach free-text questions about today's plan, readiness, or your last workout.
- Every AI output passes through validators so it can only restate what the deterministic engine already computed.
- Model weights download on demand and are excluded from backups. No inference happens in the cloud.

### Siri and Shortcuts
- Ask Siri for today's workout, your readiness, your last session, or to start a workout.
- Schedule a training window into your calendar from a suggested free block.
- App Intents entities for workouts and plans, so Siri knows what is on screen.

### Apple Watch
- Standalone-capable companion: log sets, control the rest timer, and see live metrics from your wrist.
- Live heart rate during sessions written back to Apple Health as a workout.
- Watch complications and widgets for the active session.

### Progress and history
- Interactive monthly calendar and a chronological feed of sessions with volume, duration, and PR markers.
- Per-exercise charts for weight, 1RM, and volume over any date range.
- Illustrated exercise library with body-part and equipment filters, plus custom exercises.
- Muscle heatmaps across your training history.
- Form-check and equipment recognition from a photo or short clip (beta, behind a feature flag).

### Friends
- Optional account with Sign in with Apple or email.
- Share a session card, give kudos, join challenges, and see leaderboards.
- A web view of shared sessions and challenges at [cresca-ai.app/friends](https://cresca-ai.app/friends), no app required to look.
- Sharing is opt-in per session. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.

### Privacy
- Local-first storage with optional iCloud private-database sync.
- No third-party analytics SDKs, no ads, no data sale.
- CSV export and permanent delete of all data from the Profile tab.
- Account deletion available in-app for the optional Friends features.

## How to use it

1. **Get the beta.** Request a TestFlight invite at [cresca-ai.app](https://cresca-ai.app). Requires iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch features require watchOS 11 or later.
2. **First run.** A short onboarding walks through units, rest-timer defaults, and optional Apple Health access. The AI model downloads in the background over Wi-Fi.
3. **Train.** Start a blank session or pick a routine from the Train tab. Tap a set to log it, enter weight and reps on the keypad, and rate effort when prompted. The rest timer starts automatically.
4. **Finish.** Complete the workout to see the recap, PRs, and an optional share card.
5. **Review.** The History and Progress tabs hold the calendar, charts, heatmaps, and per-exercise detail.
6. **Ask the coach.** Open the Coach tab for today's plan and readiness, or ask Siri "What's my workout today in Cresca."
7. **Go social (optional).** Sign in from Profile to add friends, join challenges, and share sessions.

## How it is built

- Swift and SwiftUI, MVVM, iOS 17+ minimum. Observation framework view models, structured concurrency throughout.
- SwiftData as the local system of record, CloudKit private database for optional sync.
- HealthKit, ActivityKit, WidgetKit, App Intents, WatchConnectivity.
- On-device AI through Apple Foundation Models and MLX Swift. Quantized Gemma weights are served from a static CDN with a signed manifest.
- Optional accounts and social features on a serverless backend behind a strict row-level-security boundary. The backend never receives your training log unless you share a session.
- Deterministic analysis engines for progression, strain, and recovery live in a shared module used by both the phone and the watch.

The repository holding this code is private. Reach out at **support@cresca-ai.app** if you would like a walkthrough of the architecture, the AI grounding pipeline, the test suite, or the product documentation.
